## 💡 Tips for Best Results

### Lighting Matters
Good lighting helps the tracking work better. Make sure you're in a well-lit room with light coming from in front of you, not behind you.

### Wear Contrasting Clothing
Simple, solid colors work best. Avoid wearing the same color as your background.

### Keep Your Full Body Visible
The tracking works best when your entire body is in frame. Stand at a distance that shows your whole body from head to toe.

### Move Smoothly
While the tracking is fast, smooth movements give cleaner results than jerky ones.
